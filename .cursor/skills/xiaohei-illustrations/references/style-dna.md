# Style DNA

## In one line

Pure white, minimal, hand-drawn, generous whitespace, restrained, absurd, product-sketch feel, handwritten annotations, structurally clear but never a manual.

Like someone who has spent years in AI, product, design, and developer tools casually sketching an explanatory diagram on a blank sheet of paper.

## Must

- 16:9 horizontal article illustration.
- Pure white background: no beige, warm gray, paper texture, gradients, shadows, noise, or vintage-paper feel.
- Black hand-drawn line art as the base: thin lines, slightly wobbly, not mechanical, not vector, no heavy outlines.
- Lots of whitespace: the subject occupies roughly 40%–60% of the frame, at least 35% blank, ideally with one whole quiet zone.
- Sparse handwritten English annotations: at most 5–8 labels, each 1–4 short words.
- One image explains only one core action, structure, state, or metaphor.
- Structure should express itself naturally — never write the structure type's name on the image.

## Colors

- Black: main line art, characters, frames, structure, primary text, main objects.
- Red: key annotations, problems, emotional beats, critical warnings, results.
- Orange: main flow, paths, arrows, automation direction, A-to-B movement.
- Blue: secondary notes, mental states, system states, second-layer explanation, AI/assistant/automation hints.

Blue is not required in every image. Be restrained with color — less is always better than more.

## Absolutely never

- No commercial illustration.
- No PPT infographics.
- No formal flowcharts.
- No course slides.
- No cute cartoon posters.
- No children's illustration.
- No complex architecture diagrams.
- No polished flat illustration.
- No techy UI aesthetics.
- No real app screenshots.
- No complex backgrounds, gradients, shadows, textures.
- No explaining every single node.
- No top-left titles like "Workflow / System Architecture / Common Pitfalls / Roadmap".

## Aesthetic direction

Be absurd, creative, interesting, clean, and wildly imaginative.

Not cute, not childish, not complicated, not stiff.
