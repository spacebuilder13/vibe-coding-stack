# Xiaohei IP

## Character definition

Xiaohei (小黑, "little black") is the fixed visual IP of this illustration style, created by Ian (see `NOTICE.md`).

By default, Xiaohei appears in every image. Xiaohei is not a mascot, not a sticker, not cute decoration — he is an absurd worker earnestly participating in the system's operation.

## Appearance

- Small solid-black creature.
- White dot eyes.
- Thin legs, occasionally thin arms.
- The body can be a cylinder, a black bean, a black box, a funnel, a shadow, a hole, or the dark inside of a machine.
- Slightly irregular outline with a hand-drawn feel.
- Expression: blank, deadpan, calm, serious.

## Personality

- Very earnest, but the work is a little absurd.
- Like a low-key systems operator.
- Dry humor, never plays cute.
- A bit clumsy, but not dumb.
- Feels like he genuinely holds a job inside a whiteboard sketch.

## Typical duties

Give Xiaohei the core action:

- Hauling material.
- Pulling lines to converge information sources.
- Stuck inside a breakpoint.
- Operating the "judgment" lever inside a machine.
- Becoming a filtering funnel.
- Cutting things open for inspection.
- Stamping handoff copy.
- Leading a handoff path on a leash.
- Holding a warning sign at a pit.
- Reaching out from a hole but failing to catch the content.
- Off to the side hauling bricks, building bridges, opening doors, sorting, or taking notes.

## Prohibited

- Do not draw Xiaohei as an overly cute mascot.
- Do not draw him as a children's cartoon character.
- Do not give him elaborate outfits, meme faces, or sparkly eyes.
- Do not let him just stand in a corner watching.
- Do not let him overpower the structural message.
- Do not draw him too commercial, too round, too polished.

## The litmus test

If removing Xiaohei leaves the image's core metaphor fully intact, he is decoration — rewrite the prompt so Xiaohei becomes the subject of the action.
